# The Northern Kingdom

The Northern Kingdom is a vast realm stretching from the Frostspine Mountains to the Endless Sea.

## Geography

The landscape is dominated by snow-capped peaks, deep valleys, and ancient evergreen forests.

## Climate

Harsh winters last nine months, with brief but intense summers.

## People

The Northerners are a hardy folk, known for their resilience and martial traditions.
