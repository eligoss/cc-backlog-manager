# The Great War

The Great War was a devastating conflict that engulfed the entire known world two centuries ago.

## Origins

The war began when the Northern Kingdom claimed sovereignty over the neutral trade routes.

## Major Battles

The Battle of Crimson Fields was the turning point, where allied forces united against the Northern aggression.

## Resolution

The Treaty of Eternal Peace was signed, establishing the current borders and political order.
