# Alex

Alex is the protagonist of our story, an ordinary person with an extraordinary destiny.

## Background

Before awakening in the forest, Alex lived a mundane life as a librarian in a small town. They had no knowledge of magic, other worlds, or their true heritage.

## Personality

Curious to a fault, Alex cannot resist a mystery. This trait will both save and endanger them throughout the story.

## Character Arc

Alex begins as someone defined by others' expectations. Through the trials ahead, they will discover their own identity and purpose.
