# Laws of Physics

The world operates under consistent physical laws similar to our own, with notable exceptions in areas touched by magic.

## Gravity

Gravity functions normally at 1g, pulling all objects toward the ground.

## Time

Time flows linearly and at a constant rate for all observers. There is no time dilation or manipulation possible, even through magic.

## Conservation

Energy and matter are conserved in all non-magical interactions. Magic can temporarily circumvent these laws but at a cost.
