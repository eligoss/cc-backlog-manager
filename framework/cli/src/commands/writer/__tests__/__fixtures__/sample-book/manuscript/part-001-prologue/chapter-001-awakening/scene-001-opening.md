# Opening Scene

The protagonist awakens in an unfamiliar forest clearing, mist swirling around their prone form.

Their head throbs with a dull ache, and as they push themselves upright, fragments of memory slip away like water through fingers.

"Where am I?" The question escapes their lips, absorbed by the dense fog.

The forest is silent save for the occasional drip of moisture from ancient branches. Something feels wrong - the very air seems to hum with an energy they cannot name.

In the distance, a twig snaps.
